# My Favorite Series: *Dororo*

> A story about how far people will go for **wealth**, **power**, and **authority**.

---

## 📖 About
**Dororo** is a dark fantasy anime set in Japan's Sengoku period.
A lord named *Daigo Kagemitsu* makes a deal with demons for power, and offers his own newborn son, **Hyakkimaru**, as the price.

## Main Characters
- **Hyakkimaru** – fights demons to get his body back
- **Dororo** – a young thief who travels with him
- **Daigo Kagemitsu** – the lord who chose ~~family~~ power

##  Themes
1. Greed and selfish desire
2. The cost of power
3. What it means to be human
